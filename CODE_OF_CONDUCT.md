# Community code of conduct

VelarOS Platform is committed to a professional, welcoming, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when reviewing difficult technical decisions.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not a person's background or ability.
- Give actionable feedback and make uncertainty explicit.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, licenses, and attribution.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project's goals and documented boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, and knowingly unsafe or deceptive contributions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through the VelarOS-AI organization. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and free from conflicts of interest.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
